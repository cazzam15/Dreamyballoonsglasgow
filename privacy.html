This explains what personal data this website collects and how it's used. It covers dreamyballoonsglasgow.com, including the Lux Lights Glasgow page.

Who's responsible

Dreamy Balloons Glasgow, based in Glasgow, United Kingdom, is responsible for the information below. Contact: dreamyballoonsglasgow@hotmail.com.

When you enquire or book

If you use the enquiry form, we receive your name, email address, phone number, event date and the details you write. The form is delivered to our email by Formspree. If you text, WhatsApp, email or message us on social media instead, we receive your name, phone number or account, and what you tell us about your event. We use it only to reply, quote and arrange your booking. We keep it only as long as needed for your booking and our business records.

Analytics and cookies

  • Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…, lasting up to 2 years), so it only runs if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Reject", it never loads. It records the pages you view, your device and browser, your approximate location, and a random ID kept in the cookie. It's set up for statistics only, with no advertising features. The legal basis is your consent. Google keeps this data for 14 months.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics doesn't set cookies or follow you across other sites. It only records things like which page was viewed, the site you came from, and your browser and country. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are useful.

You can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings. Choosing "Reject" removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site. Your browser also remembers your cookie choice, which isn't personal data.

Who else handles data

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and keeps standard web request logs, including your IP address.
  • Formspree receives enquiry form messages and passes them to our email.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts, which reveals your IP address to Google as it does on most websites.
  • Google (Google Analytics, only if you accept) and Cloudflare process the visit statistics above.
  • If you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, that company's own privacy policy also applies.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Those transfers are covered by the UK's approved safeguards (the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the UK-US data bridge where it applies). Your data is never sold.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing dreamyballoonsglasgow@hotmail.com. If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
